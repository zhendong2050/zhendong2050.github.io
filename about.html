About

Research background, academic experience, and contact information.

Zhen Dong is an Associate Professor and PhD Supervisor at Fudan University. His research in program analysis and software testing addresses the reliability of mobile applications and robotic software. Representative work includes time-travel testing of Android apps and PID parameter specification inference for flight-control programs, which received ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards at ICSE 2020 and ISSTA 2025, respectively.

His current focus is trustworthy AI-enabled systems: robustness and task-level authorization for mobile GUI agents, and execution reliability for vision-language-action (VLA) models. He combines analysis and testing with learning-based methods, including targeted post-training, to study and improve agent reliability.

He received his PhD from Heidelberg University and was previously a Senior Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore. He serves on the editorial boards of Automated Software Engineering and the Journal of Software: Evolution and Process, and on program committees for ICSE, FSE, ASE, and ISSTA.
